# Sales Performance & Business Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze sales performance and identify business trends across different dimensions.

The dashboard provides insights into **sales, profit, quantity, categories, sub-categories, customer segments, regions, states, years, and quarters**.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Power BI Service

## 📈 Key Analysis

- Total Sales, Profit, and Quantity
- Sales and Profit by Year
- Sales by Year and Quarter
- Sales by State and Region
- Sales and Profit by Category and Sub-Category
- Sales Performance by Customer Segment
- Quantity and Sales Analysis
- Regional and State-Level Performance

## 📋 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Dashboard

Provides an overview of overall business performance using KPI cards and interactive visualizations.

**Key Metrics:**

- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity Sold
- Sales and Profit by Year
- Sales by Year and Quarter
- Sales by State
- Sales and Profit by Segment
- Profit and Sales by Category

### 2. Region and State Level Analysis

Analyzes sales and performance across different regions, states, and categories.

**Includes:**

- Category and Year filters
- Sales by State and Category
- Profit by Year and Category
- Profit and Quantity by Category and Sub-Category
- Sales by Customer Segment

### 3. Category and Sub-Category Level Analysis

Provides detailed analysis of product categories and sub-categories.

**Includes:**

- Sales and Profit by Region and Segment
- Quantity and Sales by Customer Segment
- Sales and Quantity by Sub-Category
- Profit by Year and Quarter
- Sales by Year and Quarter

## 🎯 Key Insights

The dashboard helps analyze:

- Sales and profit trends over time
- Category and sub-category performance
- Regional and state-level sales performance
- Customer segment contribution to overall sales
- Quarterly sales and profit trends
- Quantity and sales distribution across segments
